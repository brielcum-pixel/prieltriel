Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What I collect

When you use the contact form, your message opens in your own email app and is sent to hello@alvinhenrypriel.com. That email contains the name, email address, project type, and message you wrote. This website stores nothing itself: there is no database, no account system, and no newsletter list.

How I use it

Your inquiry is used for one purpose: to reply to you about your project. I do not sell, rent, or share your details with third parties for marketing.

How long I keep it

Project emails are kept as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any work that follows. You can ask me to delete your message at any time by writing to the same address.

Your rights

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your inquiry by emailing hello@alvinhenrypriel.com. I will respond personally.

Third parties

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical data under Google's own policy. Social links in the footer lead to external platforms governed by their policies.
