What is collected

When you submit the consultation form, you provide your name, contact details, tattoo idea, and any optional details such as meaning, style, placement, size, and reference file names. Reference files you select stay on your device unless a connected submission method is enabled. See site.config.js for the current submission setup.

How it is used

Your details are used for one purpose: to review your idea and reply about a consultation. Details are never sold and never shared for marketing.

How long details are kept

Inquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any work that follows. You can ask for your message to be deleted at any time through the contact email shown on the site.

Your choices

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your inquiry. You can also choose to send fewer details and add more later during the consultation.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical data under Google policy. If social links are added later, those platforms are governed by their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email listed on the contact section. Back to home.
