05 · The Custom Process

From first idea to finished tattoo.

A clear sequence. You always know what happens next, what Dave needs from you, and what you receive in return.

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Send the concept in your own words, plus any references, photos, or sketches. Rough is fine. A sentence about what it means helps more than a perfect image.

    You provide: idea, meaning, reference images if you have them

  2. 02

    Discuss meaning, style, placement and size

    A direct conversation about what the tattoo represents, the style direction, where it will sit on the body, and the approximate size. Placement and scale shape the design from the start.

    You provide: preferred style, placement, approximate size

  3. 03

    Develop the custom concept

    Dave draws the custom direction from the consultation notes. Composition, linework, and shading are planned for your body and for clean healing.

    You receive: a custom concept based on your consultation

  4. 04

    Finalize the direction

    You review the concept together, refine placement and scale, and confirm the final direction before anything is scheduled for skin.

    You receive: confirmed design direction and session plan

  5. 05

    Book the tattoo session

    Once the direction is agreed, the session is scheduled. Aftercare guidance is covered so the piece settles and heals as intended.

    You receive: session date and aftercare guidance
